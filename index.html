Introduction

The Mercedes Benz C63 AMG W204 is regarded to be the one of the best high-performance sports saloons that’s on the market in past 18 years. The tag AMG meaning is Aufrecht, Melcher, and Großaspach (Wikipedia 2021) which signifies 3 things: A for Hans Werner Aufrecht which is one of the main engineers who founded Mercedes, B for Erhard Melcher which is the second engineer which specifies on high-performance engines such as the C63, finally G stands Großaspach which is where all this started. All these makes us see why AMG engines such wonders they’re as built for purely for this purpose. C63 AMG became popular between 2008 to 2014 as the naturally aspirated 6.2-litre V8 engine which delivered such an amazing power and torque, unlike other cars these engines use naturally aspirated engine giving distinctive performance characteristics. From a mechanical engineering perspective, C63 is an interesting example as how does a naturally aspirated engine design, transmission, suspension and vehicle dynamics that is carefully designed to produce a high-performance vehicle. However, such a magnificent engineering presents cons: fuel consumption, expensive maintenance and rear wheel drive traction limitations.
